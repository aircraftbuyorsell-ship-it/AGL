# Questions for the seller

Pick the questions that matter for this aircraft, tailor them, and group them by topic so the user can send them as a message. Ask for documents (scans) rather than summaries where possible.

## Identity and ownership

- What is the registration mark and the airframe, engine and propeller serial numbers?
- Are you the registered owner? If not, what is your authority to sell?
- Are there any liens, loans, leases or other claims on the aircraft?
- Why are you selling, and how long have you owned it?

## Records

- Are all airframe, engine and propeller logbooks available back to new? Can you send complete scans?
- Are any periods missing from the logbooks? Why?
- Can you send the AD compliance list and the Form 337s or repair and modification approvals?
- Which STCs and major modifications are installed, and is the paperwork complete for each?
- Is the weight and balance current?

## History

- Has the aircraft ever been damaged or involved in an accident or incident, however minor?
- Has it ever had a prop strike, gear-up landing, hard landing or sudden engine stoppage?
- Has it ever been used for training, hire, towing, skydiving or aerial work?
- Where has it been based and hangared? Has it been stored for long periods?
- Any insurance claims?

## Engine and propeller

- Time since major overhaul, the date of the overhaul and who performed it?
- Any cylinder replacements or top overhauls? When and why?
- What is the oil consumption? Are there oil analysis reports?
- When was the last compression test, and what were the results by cylinder?
- Propeller time and date since overhaul?
- Is an engine monitor installed? Can you share recent data?

## Condition and maintenance

- When was the last annual, 100-hour or ARC inspection, and who performed it?
- Any known squawks or deferred items?
- Any corrosion found or treated in the past?
- What avionics are installed? Is it compliant with the equipment requirements where I intend to fly (for example ADS-B Out in the US or 8.33 kHz radios in Europe)?

## Deal terms

- Will you allow an independent pre-buy inspection at a shop of my choosing, including a test flight?
- Who pays to correct airworthiness discrepancies found during the pre-buy?
- Which escrow or title company do you propose, and are you open to one I select?
- Where will the aircraft be delivered, and when is it available for inspection?
