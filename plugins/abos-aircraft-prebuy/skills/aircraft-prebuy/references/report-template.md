# Pre-buy report template

Use this structure for a full evaluation. Write the report in the user's language. Omit sections that do not apply and say why if that is not obvious. Keep it scannable: tables for data, short bullets for findings.

---

## Pre-buy assessment: [Make Model], [Registration], S/N [serial]

**Prepared:** [date] · **Based on:** [what the user provided: listing, logbook scans, inspection report, conversation]

> This assessment organises the information provided. It is not an inspection, an airworthiness determination, a valuation, or legal or tax advice. Have a licensed, independent inspector examine the aircraft and its records before purchase.

### 1. Recommendation

One of:

- **Proceed to pre-buy inspection**: nothing found so far that should stop the deal.
- **Proceed with conditions**: worth pursuing once the listed items are resolved.
- **Do not proceed yet**: one or more Stop findings must be resolved first.
- **Walk away**: serious, unresolvable problems or strong signs of fraud.

Then two or three sentences on why.

### 4. Still unknown

A list of the information that is missing and matters, with who can provide it.

### 5. Must verify independently

- AD applicability and compliance for the exact serial numbers, in the official FAA or EASA database
- Registered owner and liens, through the registry and a title or legal professional
- Airworthiness, through the pre-buy inspection
- Any items specific to this aircraft

### 7. Cost drivers and negotiation points
