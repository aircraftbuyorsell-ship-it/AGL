# Privacy Policy: ABOS Aircraft Pre-Buy Advisor

Last updated: 2026-10-15

ABOS Aircraft Pre-Buy Advisor ("the plugin") is published by Aircraft Buy Or Sell (ABOS).

## What the plugin is

The plugin is text only: one skill made of instructions and reference documents that Claude reads. It contains no executable code, hooks, MCP servers, analytics or tracking.

## Data the plugin collects

None. The plugin does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data, aircraft data or usage data. ABOS receives nothing from your use of the plugin.

## Data you share with Claude

Anything you type or upload while the plugin is active, such as listing text, logbook excerpts, registration marks or names, is processed by Claude under the terms and privacy policy of the Claude product you are using, operated by Anthropic. The plugin does not send that content anywhere else.

Logbooks and aircraft documents can contain personal data of owners and mechanics, such as names and certificate numbers. Share only what you need for the review, and remove personal details you do not want processed.

## Children

The plugin is intended for adults involved in buying or selling aircraft. It is not directed at people under 18.

## Changes

Changes to this policy are published in this file in the public repository, together with a new plugin version.
