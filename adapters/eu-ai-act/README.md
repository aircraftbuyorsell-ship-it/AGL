# EU AI Act → AGL Adapter

Maps versioned EU AI Act legal references into AGL governance evidence.

The adapter records:
- regulation/version;
- article or annex reference;
- applicability assessment state;
- implementation/mapping state;
- source reference to EUR-Lex.

Categories are intentionally descriptive: prohibited, transparency, high-risk, GPAI, governance, or other.

AGL does **not** determine whether a system is legally compliant or whether a provision applies. Legal applicability remains a separate assessment performed by the responsible organization and qualified legal/compliance process.

The source of truth for the regulation is EUR-Lex. AGL stores references and evidence rather than reproducing the legal text.
