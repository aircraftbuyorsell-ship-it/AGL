# AGL ↔ A2A

A2A provides interoperability between agents.

AGL does not replace A2A.

AGL adds governance to agent-to-agent delegation and execution:

- agent identity
- capability scope
- delegation chain
- authorization
- policy evaluation
- approval gates
- escalation
- execution evidence
- audit and replay

The key boundary is:

**A2A enables agent-to-agent collaboration. AGL governs the authority, constraints, and evidence of that collaboration.**
